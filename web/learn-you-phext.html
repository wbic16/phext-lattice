Learn You Phext

An introduction to phext that is a phext: plain text cut into scrolls, every scroll at a coordinate a.b.c/d.e.f/g.h.i inside its own file. The table of contents below is parsed from the phext itself, in your browser, with the same delimiter rules as the reference parser — pick a coordinate and read exactly that scroll, nothing else. That is the everyday phext move: toc to navigate, select to read.

Coordinates are internal to a phext: the scheme is shared, the addresses are local to each file. This phext is non-personal teaching material, written for anyone.
